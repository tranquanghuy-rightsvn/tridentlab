Products

At trident dental lab we utilise a digital approach wherever possible. Not only does this allow us to plan and manufacture cases at a high level, but it also allows us to keep our costs much lower than with older, more labour-intensive techniques. With the high quality of materials that are available for use in a digital workflow today, this can be accomplished without ever needing to compromise on quality.

With that in mind, quality is not the only important factor. It doesn't matter how technically perfect the design, material and fit is, if it doesn't look incredible. Sometimes having the artistic touch of a master technician can bring a case to life. We are committed to being able to provide for even the most aesthetically demanding cases and have the option of adding ceramic layering by our skilled technicians on any case.

Trident Dental Labs was started with a focus on aesthetic fixed prosthodontic solutions including implant restorations, however we are constantly looking for ways to expand to better support dentists, so please don't hesitate to contact us if you'd like to discuss any specific needs.
