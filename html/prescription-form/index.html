How to Send a Case

  1. Download & Complete the Form

    Fill in patient details, restoration type, materials, shade and requested delivery date. Add any notes for the technician in the comments field.

  2. Prepare Your Scan or Impression

    Export your intraoral scan as an STL file, or prepare the physical impression / model to be sent to the lab.

  3. Email Everything to the Lab

    Attach the completed prescription form and your scan file, then send to admin@tridentdentallab.com.au. For physical impressions, courier or drop them at the lab with a printed copy of the form.
