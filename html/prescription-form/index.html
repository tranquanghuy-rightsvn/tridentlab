      How to Send a Case

      1. Download & Complete the Card

        Fill in dentist & practice details, patient name, type of prosthesis, material, design and shade. Add any notes for the technician under Additional Instructions.

      2. Prepare Your Scan or Impression

        Export your intraoral scan as an STL file, or prepare the physical impression / model to be sent to the lab.

      3. Email Everything to the Lab

        Attach the completed prescription card and your scan file, then send to cases.thetridentlab@gmail.com. For physical impressions, courier or drop them at the lab with a printed copy of the card.
