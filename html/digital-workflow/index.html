✦ Digital Workflow

Scan to Seat,
Fully Digital

Technology plays a huge role in modern dental lab work. A digital-first workflow lets us plan and assess every case at a high level, while keeping turnaround fast and costs competitive.

The Process

From Scan to Delivery

Every case follows the same disciplined digital sequence — whether it’s a single crown or a full-arch rehabilitation.

01

Intraoral / Model Scan

We accept STL files from most intraoral scanners, or physical impressions and models sent to the lab.

02

Digital Design (CAD)

Technicians design the restoration digitally, checking occlusion, contacts and aesthetics before manufacture.

03

Precision Manufacture (CAM)

Milling, printing and pressing to fine tolerances, followed by hand-finishing and ceramic layering where required.

04

Quality Check & Dispatch

Every case is checked against the prescription before being packed and sent back to your practice.

Why Digital, Why Now

A digital workflow allows us to plan and manufacture cases at a high level, while keeping costs much lower than older, more labour-intensive techniques. With the quality of materials available today, this comes without ever needing to compromise on the final result.

Digital planning also means fewer remakes, faster turnaround, and clearer communication between your practice and our technicians — you can see exactly what’s been planned before it’s manufactured.
