✦ Materials & Brands

Materials Chosen
for the Result

We cater for most common restoration materials and digital systems, matched to what each case actually needs — not a one-size-fits-all default.

IPS e.max

Lithium disilicate glass-ceramic, valued for its strength-to-aesthetics balance in anterior and posterior crowns.

Multi-layer Zirconia

Graduated shade and translucency through the layer for a natural result in high-strength full-contour restorations.

Zirconia

High-strength framework and full-contour zirconia for crowns, bridges and implant-supported restorations.

Ceramic Layering

Hand-layered porcelain over a zirconia or metal substructure for the most aesthetically demanding cases.

CEREC Digital Design

Chairside and lab-side CAD/CAM compatibility for same-visit and lab-finished restorations.

Denture Acrylics

Quality acrylic and flexible denture materials for full and partial dentures built for comfort and durability.

Implant Components

Compatible abutments and components across major implant systems for implant-supported restorations.

Occlusal Splint Materials

Hard, soft and dual-laminate splint materials for occlusal splints and temporary solutions.

Shade Systems

Digital and conventional shade matching to support precise, repeatable shade selection on every case.

Brand and product names are used to describe material and system compatibility only and do not imply exclusive partnership or endorsement. If your case calls for a specific material or brand not listed here, contact us to confirm compatibility before sending your prescription.
